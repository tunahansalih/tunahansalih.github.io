Ph.D. research

Aug 2023 - Present Virginia Tech Graduate Research Assistant

  • Reproduced RL post-training with reward-free activation steering on frozen DiTs: 90.0–96.3% of reward gains on six image models, 89.8% / 111.9% on two video models (65× fewer parameters, SD3.5-M).
  • Reused DiT attention parameters (ConceptAttention, ICML 2025 Oral) for training-free concept saliency: 76.45 zero-shot mIoU on Pascal VOC vs. 64.33 for the best baseline.
  • Improved object-attribute binding (CONFORM, CVPR 2024) and composed multiple LoRAs at test time (CLoRA, ICCV 2025 Highlight; user study 3.32 vs. 2.81 out of 5), both via contrastive objectives.
  • Transferred video motion without fine-tuning via attention-driven latent trajectory guidance across UNet and DiT models (MotionFlow, AAAI 2026); top-rated on all criteria in a 50-participant user study.
  • Cut per-token KV-cache memory by 92.7% with low-rank caching for autoregressive video (VideoMLA, NeurIPS 2026), ranking first overall among evaluated methods on long-horizon (60-second) VBench.
  • Generated 10-minute action-controllable videos autoregressively via RoPE re-indexing (Infinity-RoPE, CVPR 2026).
  • Aligned VAE latent geometry via fixed-radius shell projection and spherical flow matching: SiT-B/2 ImageNet-256 FID 13.83 to 6.88 (50.2% lower) vs. the matched linear-flow baseline.
