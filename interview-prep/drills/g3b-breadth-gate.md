# G3b breadth gate

Use 45 seconds per question. A partner should read the questions, or use a
repeating timer. Do not open the answer cues until all 15 are complete.

1. Name three positional-encoding families and one tradeoff of each.
2. What does "5D parallelism" usually refer to, and what does each dimension split?
3. What is the operational difference between PPO and GRPO?
4. Why does DETR not need NMS?
5. How do epsilon prediction and v prediction differ?
6. Give the attention-cost expressions for joint and factorized space-time attention.
7. What is APH, and what does it add to AP?
8. Why does ordinary classifier-free guidance often double the inference batch?
9. What does `shard_map` control?
10. Why is transformer prefill usually compute-bound while decode is memory-bound?
11. State the conditional-flow-matching target.
12. Give one way a model can game a VBench metric.
13. Why does an MoE need load balancing?
14. How does KV-cache memory scale with batch, sequence length, layers, and KV heads?
15. What is an in-batch false negative in a contrastive loss?
