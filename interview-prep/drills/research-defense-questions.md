# Research-defense questions

Use questions 1-6 for G10. Use 7-12 for later retests.

1. What was your personal contribution, separated from the team’s contribution?
2. What was the strongest baseline, and why was it the correct comparison?
3. Which uncontrolled confound is most capable of explaining the headline result?
4. What concrete result would falsify the paper’s main claim?
5. What is the most consequential limitation that the paper understates?
6. Which approach failed, and what evidence—not intuition—made you stop?
7. Why is this a research contribution rather than an engineering improvement?
8. Which evaluation metric is easiest to game in this paper?
9. What changes if compute or data increases by 10x?
10. What breaks first under domain shift?
11. Which reviewer criticism was hardest to answer?
12. If you reran only one experiment today, which one and why?
